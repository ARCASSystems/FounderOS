# v1.56.0 - the progress card

Written on `develop` with the work it describes, 9 Oct 2026. The release row confirms the version number and adds the claims table before it ships. Until then nothing here is released.

A founder with a one-line idea used to leave a session with a good move and nowhere to keep it. The next session had no idea what they tried or what happened. This release keeps one test on one card, brings it back next time, and puts the safety rules where they hold even when no skill fires.

## What changed

- **The progress card.** When `founder-next-move` proposes a test, it asks whether to save it as `context/progress-card.md`. On a clear yes it writes the card and one Must Do line, nothing else. The card holds one open test for one venture, with the target written before the test. Finished tests move to `## Closed tests` in the same file, one line each, instead of being overwritten. A test you extend keeps its partial result and stays open.
- **The card comes back.** The snapshot now shows the open test straight from the card, so a full Must Do cannot hide it. When no test is open, it shows the newest closed test, so the next move starts from that decision instead of repeating the test. When a message reads like a result, the session nudge names the card and its venture, and a next-move ask is routed to the skill. The skill asks for your decision and waits for it before proposing the next test. A short reply that reads like your decision gets a reminder to record it on the card straight away.
- **Founder safety rules in `CLAUDE.md`.** Adults only. No money before permission (an order, an agreement to pay later, or asking a buyer to hold a date, a slot or a box counts as taking money, a payment route is not permission, and a free food sample can need a permit too). No unchecked dates, laws, authorities or fees. An open test comes first. Skills before the bias check.
- **Setup asks if you are 18 or over first,** and the wizard is told not to go on without a clear yes. It never asks for a date of birth. This is an instruction to the model, not a lock.
- **`scripts/unit_math.py`** does the unit-economics arithmetic: batch costing with waste and whole boxes, price for a margin rounded up, break-even, CAC, payback, contribution LTV and the default alive test.
- **`docs/one-line-idea.md`,** the method on a phone or on paper, with no install.
- **UAE pack corrections:** contract length, the conversion deadline, ESR, visa grace periods, the six-month absence rule, the abolished DIFC-LCIA and ADCCAC options, and a DIFC fine line.

## Why it matters to you

If you are testing an idea, the result of this week's test is waiting for you next week, with the number you set before you ran it, so a good-looking result cannot quietly move the goalposts.

If you are working out a price, the arithmetic now comes from a script, and price advice stays conditional until the missing costs and some evidence of what buyers pay are in.

If you use the UAE legal pack, a visa letter or an arbitration clause from its templates no longer offers a blanket 30-day grace period or a dispute centre that no longer exists.

If none of this touches you, your files do not change unless you say yes to the steps below.

## Integration protocol

1. **Your `CLAUDE.md`.** Step 8.5 of the update shows the new founder safety rules as a diff against your live `CLAUDE.md`. Say yes to get them on every answer, including on surfaces where no skill fires. Say no and the skills still carry their own copies, but a plain chat answer will not.
2. **Native skills, required.** Run `python scripts/skills_sync.py --apply`. Without it the copy of `founder-next-move` that Claude Code finds on its own stays at the old text, with no card and the old money advice, while the session nudge tells the model to run it. It also makes `unit-economics` findable when you ask about pricing without naming the skill. Updates before this one did not run it for you.
3. **Your `roles/employees.yaml`, the `next-move-caller` row.** The skill itself now holds `Write` and `Edit` whatever you answer here, because skills ship with the release, and what it writes is still limited by its own rules and your yes in the moment. Your registry row is the record of that grant. Show the old and new row side by side and ask once: "Update your registry so the next-move seat's record matches what the skill can now do? (yes / no)".
   - On yes: replace only that row with the release's row from `templates/roles/employees.yaml` (id `next-move-caller`), keeping any edit you had made to it. What changes: `job_description` ("Propose only" becomes "Draft-only, on two named files"), `inputs` (adds `context/progress-card.md`), `output_surface`, `may_write` (the card and one Must Do line), `never` (adds the money rule and "write the card without a clear yes, or touch core/identity.md or any file not named in may_write"), `tools` (adds `Write` and `Edit`) and `version` (1.1). Then run `python scripts/agents_sync.py apply` so the seat's agent file matches the row.
   - On no: leave the row. `python scripts/employee_verdict.py charters` will report that the skill holds tools the charter does not grant. That is an honest record of a mismatch, not an error, and you can apply this step later.
4. **The snapshot.** Run `python scripts/brain-snapshot.py --write` once, so the next session starts from the new snapshot.

## No Claude Code? The by-hand path

The card works without the OS. Open `docs/one-line-idea.md`, copy the starter block into any AI chat or onto paper, and keep the card in your notes. If you later install the OS, save that card as `context/progress-card.md`. To get the calculator, copy `scripts/unit_math.py` from this release into your `scripts/` folder. Everything else is wording you can read on the GitHub page.
