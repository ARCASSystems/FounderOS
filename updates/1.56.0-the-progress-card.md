# v1.56.0 - the progress card

A founder with a one-line idea used to leave a session with a good move and nowhere to keep it. The next session had no idea what they tried or what happened. This release keeps one test on one card, brings it back next time, and puts the safety rules where they hold even when no skill fires.

## What changed

- **The progress card.** When `founder-next-move` proposes a test, it asks to save it.
  - The question names both files: your progress card (`context/progress-card.md`) and this week's Must Do. A clear yes covers those two writes and nothing else.
  - The card holds one open test for one venture, with the target written before the test.
  - Finished tests move to `## Closed tests` in the same file, one line each, newest first.
  - A test you run for longer keeps its partial result and stays open.
- **The card comes back.**
  - The snapshot shows the open test straight from the card, so a full Must Do cannot hide it.
  - When no test is open, it shows the newest closed test, so the next move starts from that decision.
  - When a message reads like a result, the session nudge names the card.
  - When you answer the question about your decision (keep, change or stop), a reminder tells the model to record it on the card in that turn. The skill waits for your decision before it proposes the next test.
- **Six founder safety rules in `CLAUDE.md`:**
  - Adults only.
  - No money before permission. An order, an agreement to pay later, or asking a buyer to hold a date, a slot, an appointment or a box counts as taking money. A payment route is not permission, and a free food sample can need a permit too.
  - No unchecked dates, laws, authorities or fees.
  - An open test comes first.
  - Skills before the bias check.
  - A yes covers only what was named.
- **Setup asks if you are 18 or over first,** and does not go on without a clear yes. It never asks for a date of birth. This is an instruction to the model. It is not a lock.
- **`scripts/unit_math.py`** does the unit-economics arithmetic: batch costing with waste and whole boxes, a price for a margin rounded up, break-even, CAC, payback, contribution LTV, and the default alive test.
  - A percent fee, such as a delivery app's commission, goes into the price.
  - A shrinking business is followed to the month its cash runs out.
  - It refuses inputs that cannot be real, and it says every default it used.
- **`docs/one-line-idea.md`,** the method on a phone or on paper, with no install.
- **The drafting skills carry the money rule themselves:** the email drafter, the review responder, the proposal writer, the pitch deck and the scope challenge.
- **The snapshot leaves out the seeded example flag and pattern,** so a demo is never read as your own state, while the first brief still shows them as the demo.
- **UAE pack corrections:**
  - contract length and the decree's date, and the conversion deadline
  - ESR, visa grace periods and the six-month absence rule
  - the 5-year tourist visa's balance rule, which is a clarification of an existing rule
  - the abolished DIFC-LCIA and the replaced ADCCAC, and a DIFC fine line
  - MoIAT in place of ESMA

## Why it matters to you

If you are testing an idea, the result of this week's test is waiting for you next week, with the number you set before you ran it, so a good-looking result cannot quietly move the goalposts. A test you already ran is not handed back to you as new.

If you are working out a price, the arithmetic now comes from a script, a delivery app's cut is part of the price, and price advice stays conditional until the missing costs and some evidence of what buyers pay are in.

If you use the UAE legal pack, a visa letter or an arbitration clause from its templates no longer offers a blanket 30-day grace period or a dispute centre that no longer exists.

If none of this touches you, your files do not change unless you say yes to the steps below.

## What the release gate found before this shipped

Three review rounds and eight live rounds came before this pack.

- **Codex round 1** challenged the plan and found twelve gaps.
- **Codex round 2** checked the fixes and found eleven more. Two were blockers: a money ask before the founder may sell, and the age gate. All were fixed.
- **A hostile reviewer** that built none of it then found eleven more. Among them were a runway verdict that called a shrinking business alive, and drafting gates that switched off after a sale to friends.
- **Live runs.** Scripted founders ran through headless Claude Code on frozen copies of this tree: 48 conversations in rounds A to C, then 36 more in rounds D to G. Each failing round was read from the card files and the answers, fixed, pinned by a test and run again. The runs caught:
  - decisions held back unwritten
  - buyers asked to hold a date, with the answer counted as intent
  - a private note paraphrased onto a card
  - a test proposed again after it was disconfirmed
- **Three reviewers before Codex round 3** read the code, the rules and the facts. Every finding below is fixed in this pack:
  - the decision reminder firing on everyday chat
  - a card parser that misread past-tense decisions
  - a calculator with no room for a delivery app's percentage
  - consent rules that contradicted each other
  - a stale native copy of the skill that an update could leave in place
  - four facts to correct
- **Codex round 3** reviews this exact tree before it ships. Its findings, and what was done about each, are added here before release.

## Integration protocol

1. **Native skills, required, and first.** Run `python scripts/skills_sync.py --apply`.
   - Claude Code finds skills on its own through generated copies in `.claude/skills/`, and an update does not refresh them.
   - The update command you are running is the one from your old version, which has no step for this. Without it, the copy of `founder-next-move` that Claude Code finds keeps the old text, with no card and the old money advice.
   - Until you run it, the nudges name the stale copy and point the model at the current file.
   - It also makes `unit-economics` findable when you ask about pricing without naming the skill.
2. **Your `CLAUDE.md`.** Step 8.5 of the update shows the new founder safety rules as a diff against your live `CLAUDE.md`. Say yes to get them on every answer, including on surfaces where no skill fires. Say no and the skills still carry their own copies, once step 1 has run, but a plain chat answer will not.
3. **After the update finishes: your `roles/employees.yaml`, the `next-move-caller` row.** The update never writes your own files, so this is a change you ask for afterwards ("apply the next-move row from the 1.56.0 pack").
   - The skill itself holds `Write` and `Edit` whatever you answer, because skills ship with the release. What it writes is still limited by its own rules and your yes in the moment.
   - Your registry row is the record of that grant. Show the old and new row side by side and ask once: "Update your registry so the next-move seat's record matches what the skill can now do? (yes / no)".
   - On yes: replace only that row with the release's row from `templates/roles/employees.yaml` (id `next-move-caller`), keeping any edit you had made to it. What changes: `job_description` ("Propose only" becomes "Draft-only, on two named files"), `inputs` (adds `context/progress-card.md`), `output_surface`, `may_write` (the card and one Must Do line), `never` (the money rule, and no card write without a clear yes), `tools` (adds `Write` and `Edit`) and `version` (1.1). Then run `python scripts/agents_sync.py apply` so the seat's agent file matches the row.
   - On no: leave the row. `python scripts/employee_verdict.py charters` will report that the skill holds tools the charter does not grant. That is an honest record of the mismatch, and you can apply this step later.
4. **After the update finishes: the snapshot.** Ask to refresh it, or run `python scripts/brain-snapshot.py --write`, so the next session starts from the new snapshot.

## No Claude Code? The by-hand path

The card works without the OS. Open `docs/one-line-idea.md`, copy the starter block into any AI chat or onto paper, and keep the card in your notes. If you later install the OS, save that card as `context/progress-card.md`. To get the calculator, copy `scripts/unit_math.py` from this release into your `scripts/` folder. Everything else is wording you can read on the GitHub page.
