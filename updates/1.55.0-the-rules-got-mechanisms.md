# v1.55.0 - the rules got mechanisms

Three parts of this OS had the same shape of problem: a rule that existed as a page with nothing behind it. Writing quality was judged by a model and never measured. Market research was grounded in your own files and could not see the market. The privacy tier of voice was a paragraph with build instructions for an endpoint nobody had written. And the front door, after fifty-three versions, was still a link to a raw branch archive.

This release puts a mechanism behind each one, and the mechanisms turned up defects in the things they were wired into. That is the point of building them.

## What changed

- **The download is a real release.** `FounderOS-1.55.0.zip` from a tagged release, extracting to one folder called **Founder OS** that carries the ARCAS mark. `scripts/build_release.py` builds it from `git ls-files`, so the ZIP and a clone hand over the same tree and the install paths cannot drift apart. `scripts/check_download_links.py` fails the suite when VERSION and the links disagree, because a pinned link is the honest kind and the kind that rots.

- **The writing rules are counted, not judged.** `scripts/register_census.py` counts eighteen classes, each one a line of `rules/writing-style.md`, and reads the banned-word list off that page so there is one list and it is the one you can read. Wired into `pre-send-check` Check 3 and the ship gate's deterministic scan. `/founder-os:writing-score` answers the question one gate cannot: is your writing getting better.

- **Market research can see the market.** `strategic-analysis` searches and fetches, binds every fetched number to a `[SOURCED: url, retrieved date]` tag, closes with a table of what to research next, and challenges your premise before it analyses anything. When the web is unreachable it says so in one line and tags every number as an estimate instead of quietly producing a table that looks sourced.

- **Your speech can stay on this machine.** `POST /transcribe` transcribes locally with faster-whisper behind a toggle. `POST /speak` speaks locally with Piper. Both opt-in, both default off, Tier 0 unchanged and still zero-install. The page names the ears that are running, not the ears that are possible.

- **`pitch-deck`**, which challenges the story before it builds the slides, checks every number before a deck exists to show anyone, and closes with what the deck cannot yet defend.

- **`rules/outside-capability.md`**, the doctrine for adding a big capability your OS has no idea how to do without absorbing someone else's project or wiring yourself into one vendor.

## What building it broke, and what that fixed

Five defects were found by running the new mechanisms against real inputs rather than by reading code. All five now have tests that would catch them coming back.

- **`claims_check.py` could not see a market number.** A two-part decimal was filtered as a version string, so `4.2 billion` - the shape of a market size, a growth rate and a multiple - was dropped in silence. Word-form percentages never matched. A document with three untagged figures reported zero uncovered claims. This is the closing gate of the market flow.
- **The same script rejected the tagging form its own rule page teaches.** `rules/research-integrity.md` says the tag goes on its own line. The reader accepted only a tag on the same line, so following the published rule got you flagged for it.
- **`scrape.py` returned a page's JavaScript as page text,** which means a quote attributed to a page could be a variable name.
- **The voice page printed a local filesystem path,** account name included, when a recording failed to decode, which is a poor thing for the privacy tier of all things to do.
- **The voice page contradicted itself:** with local ears on, the banner said the audio never leaves the machine while the subtitle two lines above still said the browser hears.

Two published rules also turned out to be unfollowable once they were measured, and were corrected rather than enforced. "Max two hyphens per piece" fights the em-dash ban directly, since the spaced hyphen is the replacement that rule mandates: these pages run five to twenty-six per thousand words. "Contractions always" is a rule for writing that talks, not for a contract or a spec.

## Integration protocol

1. Everything arrives with the update. Nothing in your own files is touched.
2. Two new scripts are copied into `scripts/` by setup or update. Neither runs unless you call it.
3. Nothing new is installed. The local voice tier and the deck renderer ask for a package only at the moment you use them, and say so plainly rather than failing.
4. If you already had `voice/` set up, say "update Founder OS" and the new `server.py` and `index.html` land with it. The Local ears and Local voice toggles appear on the page, disabled until their dependency is present.

## No Claude Code? The by-hand path

Download the release zip, extract it, and copy the `scripts/`, `skills/`, `rules/` and `docs/` folders over your install. Your own folders - `brain/`, `context/`, `core/`, `cadence/` - are not touched by any of this.
