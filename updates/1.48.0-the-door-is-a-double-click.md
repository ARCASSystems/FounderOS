# v1.48.0 - the door is a double-click

v1.47 made the OS honest about its own past. This release fixes the front door: the distance between finding the repo and living inside it, crossed without knowing a single terminal command. It came out of one operator review that asked three plain questions - can someone download and double-click to start, what about the person who already has notes, and what does coming back after weeks feel like.

## What changed

- **Download, extract, double-click.** `Start Founder OS.bat` (Windows) and `Start Founder OS.command` (Mac) now ship in the folder. Double-click: Claude Code opens right there and the setup wizard starts talking. After setup, the same double-click just opens your OS - it is the standing front door. If Claude Code is missing it says so kindly and opens the download page; if Python is missing you hear it now, not twenty minutes into the interview. Honest limits, stated where you meet them: it cannot install Claude Code or the paid plan for you, Windows may warn once about a downloaded script, and a Mac wants right-click then Open the first time. Both files are plain text - read them in any editor.
- **Setup adopts the notes you already have.** Say "set up Founder OS inside my vault" (or name Obsidian or local files during setup) and the OS moves in NEXT TO your notes instead of starting a separate folder. Four rules hold: only missing things are created, no existing file is ever written over, every collision is asked about once, and the wizard names back what it left alone. The honest wiring: Claude reads any note you point at and your Obsidian links keep working; the OS's structured search covers the OS's own folders, and "capture this" is the bridge that files an old note's takeaways where search reads. Adopt as you go - nothing needs migrating up front.
- **Coming back after weeks reads as a welcome.** Past two weeks of quiet, the session brief compacts: how long you were away, your queue, a flags count marked "waiting, not lost", and one offer - "catch me up", or just start. Only two things stay at full volume, because they do not pause for absence: stale cadence and overdue compliance deadlines.
- **People research carries sources or says it is unverified.** Meeting prep's web research about the person across the table now works under the same claims discipline as every other researched number: sources inline in the brief, a same-name check before anything is attributed (name collisions are the classic way to walk in wrong), and "unverified - confirm on the call" where a source could not be found.
- **The synced-folder capture channel got its missing instructions.** The capture guide told you to point your phone at "your cloud-synced OS folder" without mentioning that a default install is not synced anywhere. It now walks the five-minute setup: move the folder into your cloud folder, the two-action iPhone Shortcut, the Android pairing route, and when to just use email-to-self instead.

## Why it matters to you

**If you have been recommending this to someone non-technical:** the instruction is now "download, unzip, double-click Start Founder OS". That is the whole onboarding sentence.

**If you have an Obsidian vault:** you no longer choose between your history and the OS. It moves in alongside, touches nothing, and tells you exactly what your old notes can and cannot do.

**If life pulled you away for a month:** open a session. You get a welcome and one question, not a wall of everything you did not do.

## Integration protocol

1. **Take the system-layer files.** Everything here is engine and docs. Nothing writes into your data folders.
2. **Nothing to migrate.** The start files are additive; an install without them behaves exactly as before.
3. **See it once:** close Claude Code and double-click `Start Founder OS` in your folder. Your OS opens with your session brief. That is the new default way in.

## No Claude Code? The by-hand path

Two of these are habits worth holding in any tool. When you research a person before a meeting, write the source next to every claim and check it is actually the same person - a name plus a company, not a name alone - before you repeat anything to their face. And if you keep notes in more than one system, do not migrate them all into the newest one on day one; move things over the day they start mattering, and let the rest lie where they are.
