# Needs attention

Open asks the OS has raised that only you can answer. The morning loop reads this file first and turns the top items into questions.

**What belongs here.** A question the OS genuinely cannot answer from your files: a decision it has no basis to make, a fact it cannot verify, a choice between two paths that both look valid. One line each, with the date it was raised and why it is stuck.

**What does not belong here.** Anything the OS could work out by reading. Anything that is really a task (that is `cadence/queue.md`). Anything it raised once, you answered, and it forgot - an answered row stays here marked answered, which is how you can tell the difference between a new ask and a returning one.

**Answered rows are never deleted.** Mark them with the date and your one-line answer, and leave them in place. The answered row is the record of what you decided and the reason the same question stops being asked. Move them to the Answered section below once they pile up.
